Talk abstract: 10 years ago, R. Tao et al. showed that the application of a magnetic field can reduce blood viscosity, which has not been cultivated further in detail theoretically. To build a microscopic theory in that direction, we have initiated a simple kinetic theory calculation based on the Boltzmann transport equation. We considered a simple (Newtonian) fluid containing charged particles flowing due to an electric field and we will get an expression of electrical conductivity, which can be modified due to an external magnetic field. It is the Lorentz force term in the Boltzmann transport equation, which will create this modification. For viscosity also, we can get a similar modification. Our work is in progress with a long-term future goal to explain the reduction of blood viscosity due to magnetic fields.
